Sample trip structures

Start with an idea, then adapt it

These are planning examples, not fixed bookable products. Stops change with your group, season, weather, permissions and current access.

Half day

Kanthalloor introduction

Choose a small number of accessible farms, cultivated landscapes or viewpoints without trying to cover the whole region.

  • Good for: short visits
  • Pace: focused

One day

Easy Kanthalloor day

Village and terraced landscapes, an agreed farm visit, a suitable viewpoint and time for local produce.

  • Good for: families and couples
  • Pace: relaxed

One day

Agriculture & village life

A seasonally suitable fruit or vegetable farm with optional local food, craft, weaving or lemongrass experiences arranged in advance.

  • Good for: curious travellers
  • Host confirmation needed

Two days

Kanthalloor + Marayoor

Spend one day around Kanthalloor farms and scenery, then explore appropriate Marayoor heritage and jaggery stops.

  • Good for: overnight trips
  • Two distinct destination areas

Optional extension

Authorised Chinnar nature day

Add a forest-administered activity only when the appropriate programme, guide, weather and permissions are confirmed.

  • Good for: nature interests
  • Not guaranteed

Custom

Family, senior or group plan

Reduce difficult approaches, adjust the pace and coordinate room, vehicle and pickup needs around the travellers.

  • Good for: specific comfort needs
  • Built after discussion
